## Evidence handling rule

The register is deliberately separate from the canonical evidence database. When a source becomes operational evidence, create a structured evidence record with:

- source identifier;
- source type;
- exact claim;
- population;
- intervention/exposure;
- outcome;
- evidence design;
- limitations;
- safety;
- conflicts of interest;
- publication date;
- retrieval date;
- provenance;
- evidence status.

Do not infer stronger evidence merely from repetition across sources.
