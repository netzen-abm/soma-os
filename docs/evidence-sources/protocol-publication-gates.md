# SOMA Protocol Publication Gates

## Objective

Ensure every health-management protocol is traceable, appropriately qualified,
and safe to publish across every SOMA surface.

## Gate 1 — Classification

Classify the material as one of:

- traditional knowledge;
- research finding;
- clinical guidance;
- management information;
- mechanistic hypothesis;
- dataset fact;
- expert education.

Do not collapse these categories into a single "protocol" label.

## Gate 2 — Evidence

At least one identifiable source is required for a published substantive claim.
For evidence-supported claims, assess the strength and limitations of the
available evidence.

Use multiple source classes when appropriate. Do not require PubMed when the
best evidence is a government, regulatory, institutional, traditional, or
properly governed international source.

## Gate 3 — Verification

Every published source must have a real verification destination.
Placeholder, fabricated, generic, or unverified URLs fail publication.

## Gate 4 — Safety

Review:

- contraindications;
- drug or supplement interactions;
- population-specific risks;
- uncertainty;
- escalation requirements.

Do not instruct users to stop, stagger, replace, or alter prescription
medication unless supported by appropriate clinical governance.

## Gate 5 — Management boundary

Published output may provide:

- educational information;
- management-support information;
- clinician-guided management considerations.

It must not present SOMA as diagnosing, prescribing, treating, curing, or
reversing disease.

## Gate 6 — User verification

Where supporting research exists, present:

1. study/source identity;
2. concise study summary;
3. what the evidence actually found;
4. important limitations;
5. direct verification link.

Where supporting research cannot be established, state that explicitly.

## Gate 7 — Cross-surface consistency

The same governed protocol object must be used by Web, mobile, bots, AI/RAG,
and future surfaces. A client must not weaken the evidence or safety status.

## Gate 8 — Publication state

Only `PUBLISHED` records may be presented as governed SOMA knowledge.
`UNREVIEWED` and `SOURCE_FOUND` records remain internal research material.

## Withdrawal

A published record can be withdrawn when a source is retracted, a safety
signal changes its status, provenance becomes unreliable, or a material error
is discovered.
