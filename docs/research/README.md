# SOMA-OS Research

Research documents explore scientific, technical, and conceptual questions. They inform architecture and strategy but do not automatically become product contracts.

## Research-to-system rule

```text
Research question
  → source discovery
  → provenance
  → claim extraction
  → evidence assessment
  → contradiction / limitation review
  → safety / applicability
  → decision or architecture implication
```

A research document should clearly distinguish:

- source-derived findings;
- interpretation;
- open questions;
- proposed architecture implications;
- implementation recommendations.

Research must not silently promote a hypothesis into a canonical health fact.
